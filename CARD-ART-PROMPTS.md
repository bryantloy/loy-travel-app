# New card art — prompts

34 new cards are in `card-catalog.json` with `"art": "pending"`. Until their image exists, the app shows a designed placeholder face (title, rarity, emoji, stats, quote).

To add art for a card:

1. Generate the image with the prompt below, using a few current cards as style references (and family photos as face/wardrobe references for family cards).
2. Export WebP, about 2:3 (e.g. 1024x1536), quality 82-88, 100-350 KB.
3. Upload it to `assets/cards/` with the exact filename listed. The app picks it up automatically; no code change needed.
4. Optional cleanup: delete the card's `"art": "pending"` line once the art is in.

Photo references are for identity and wardrobe, not literal recreations. Keep unrelated bystanders out of new cards.
