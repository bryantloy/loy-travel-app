# Loy Travel v0.5 — Research + Food

Incremental update for the working v0.4.1 deployment.

## New
- Explore tabs: Activities / Food
- Activity detail sheets with drive/time/effort/location context
- Official / research / operator links for major and hidden-find activities
- Molokini operator links
- Food discovery list with shared family voting
- Food filters for chicken, burgers, tacos and pizza
- Restaurant website, menu and navigation links
- Rough drive-time context from Honua Kai
- Restaurant votes included in CSV export

## Important
Drive times are planning estimates, not live traffic estimates. Re-check routes, closures, hours, menus, reservations and trail/ocean conditions near the trip.

The restaurant SQL uses broad anonymous test policies to match the current family-test architecture. Do not use this model for private reservation data or photos without adding authentication/tighter RLS.
